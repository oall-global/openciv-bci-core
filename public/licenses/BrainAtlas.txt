OpenCiv BCI-001 ships the original downloaded GLB. Runtime presentation modifications:
mirrored hemisphere display, orientation/scale/centering, new material/lighting,
and RMS-driven illumination with an illustrative channel ring and data particles.
The model and these adapted model visualizations are offered under CC BY-SA 3.0.
No endorsement by the original authors is implied.
Channel-ring positions are not anatomical coordinates. Animation does not represent
neural pathways, source localization, cognitive state or diagnosis.

0.1.4 visual adaptation: emissive contour shader, digital scan lines and sampled point cloud; no solid diffuse material. Same CC BY-SA 3.0 license applies.

2026-10-18 glossary anatomy view: reused original GLB, mirrored paired regions,
retained the brainstem and corpus callosum once, region highlighting, diffuse
lighting and translucent internal views. Same CC BY-SA 3.0 license applies.
The teaching groups may cover only atlas subregions, as described in the UI.
